**Compare January–May 2022 only, using the same month, category and currency.** The red labels and arrows below show what must match.

![The three conditions for a fair Budget versus Actual comparison](images/B1_Why_Comparable.svg)

## Follow one comparison: January Sales

| What must match? | Actual | Budget | Why this matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month | January 2022 | January 2022 | Both amounts refer to the same month. |
| Category | Sales | Sales | Both amounts measure Sales income. |
| Currency | USD | USD | Both amounts are in US dollars. |
| Amount | $5,000 | $6,000 | Actual minus Budget = **−$1,000**. |

In Excel, select **January** and **Sales** in the Actual sheet and add the amounts: **$5,000**. Find **January → Sales** in the Budget sheet: **$6,000**. Subtract the budget from actual: **$5,000 − $6,000 = −$1,000**.

The actual file contains individual transactions; the budget contains monthly category totals. **Different record counts do not prevent comparison** — add transactions into the same month-and-category totals first.

For this exercise, January–May is treated as the completed reporting period.
