| Field | Meaning | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cohort | — | Text | 2016-01 |

| Eligible90 | Has a complete 90-day observation window after first purchase. | Number | 289 |
| Repeat90 | Repeat purchase within the defined 90-day window. | Number | 13 |
