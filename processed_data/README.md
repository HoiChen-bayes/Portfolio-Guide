[← Project](../README.md) · [Source Data](../raw_data/README.md)

## What was processed, and why?

| Part | Treatment and business purpose | Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare | Map nine income/expense categories and reshape the monthly budget. | 1–2 |
| Explain | Compare matching months and reconcile category contributions. | 3–5 |
| Plan | Combine actuals with future assumptions and evaluate recovery/cash cases. | 6–7, 10 |
| Review | Prepare close exceptions and a management action list. | 8–9 |
