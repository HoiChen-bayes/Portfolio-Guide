[← Project](../README.md) · [Source Data](../raw_data/README.md)

## What was processed, and why?

| Part | Treatment and business purpose | Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Validate | Reconcile units, discounts, sales and COGS. | F1–F2 |
| Compare | Aggregate portfolio results and compare equal-length periods. | F3–F6 |
| Decide | Model discount trade-offs and prepare quarterly priorities. | F7–F10 |
| Extend | Backtest forecasts, build cash scenarios, simulate close and freeze monthly versions. | F11–F18 |
