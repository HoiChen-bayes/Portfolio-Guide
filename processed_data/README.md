## What was processed, and why?

| Part | Treatment and business purpose | Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare | Map nine income/expense categories and reshape the monthly budget. | B1–B2 |
| Explain | Compare matching months and reconcile category contributions. | B3–B5 |
| Plan | Combine actuals with future assumptions and evaluate recovery/cash cases. | B6–B7, B10 |
| Review | Prepare close exceptions and a management action list. | B8–B9 |
