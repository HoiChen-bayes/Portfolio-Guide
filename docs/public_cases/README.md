# Two companies, two forecast dates

How does dated market research change a financial scenario, and does the change help explain the eventual result? These case studies examine P&G and Travelers for April–June 2026 using information available by March 31 and May 15.

Start with [P&G](PG.md) or [Travelers](TRV.md). Each page follows numbered questions, expandable evidence, scenario reasoning and an evaluation section. All forecasts are retrospective analyst reconstructions, not company budgets or forecasts actually authored in March or May.

The comparison uses four runs for each company: March history-only, March market-informed, May history-only and May market-informed. Comparing the two May runs isolates the research adjustment on the same updated history. Comparing March history-only with May history-only isolates the new historical information. Model reruns can vary; saved, hash-checked assumption replay is the exact reproducibility path.
